## Problem and resulting behavior

A task can currently have a one-character title or a past due date. This PR
trims titles, requires at least three characters, and rejects a new or changed
due date before today's UTC date with a field-specific HTTP 400 response.
Today, future dates, and null are valid. An existing overdue date is preserved
when omitted or echoed unchanged in a PUT or PATCH, allowing other fields to
be updated. Changing it to a different past date remains invalid.

## Validation

`python manage.py test tasks.tests.test_validation tasks.tests.test_baseline`

Tests cover blank/short/long titles, trimming, today/future/past/null dates,
malformed dates, invalid updates, overdue dates omitted or echoed unchanged
in PUT/PATCH, and attempts to change an overdue date to another past date.

All 17 branch tests pass. The echoed-date regressions failed before the fix
(HTTP 400 instead of 200) and pass afterwards.
